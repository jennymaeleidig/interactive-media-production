# Corporate-Jargon Voice (satire register, four modes)

Distilled from the copy-voice analyses of four real vendors; the working distillation — voice rules, sentence shapes, and lexicons — lives entirely in this skill. This skill writes **on-register copy for the satirical product** — never reproduces any real vendor's copy, and never produces copy usable to impersonate them.

## The one idea (default mode)

The institutional smile: every sentence is calm, confident, forward-leaning — while grammar does the watching. The watcher is always the grammatical subject; the watched never acts. Trust vocabulary ("guardrails," "transparency," "accountability") is worn by the thing that needs constraining. Write at full sincerity; the dissonance between the tone and what the product is *is* the satire. No winks, no exaggeration.

## Mode selection

Pick the register by surface, not by mood:

| Surface | Mode |
|---|---|
| Marketing pages, heroes, product pages, nav (the default) | **Civic-optimist** |
| Dark ops/platform surfaces, "infrastructure" claims, security capability lists | **Imperial** |
| Stewardship surfaces — governance essays, values, "our responsibility" bands; the pages wearing the warm creams | **Plain speech** |
| Data-backed claim surfaces — price ledgers, "savings to the community" receipts, public-notice blocks, coupon-legal fine print | **Government blend** |

One surface, one mode. Mode bleed (an imperial noun in a civic-optimist sentence, a rally superlative in a receipt row) is a defect. The government blend is the one licensed exception — its engine *is* unacknowledged register switching (see Mode 4).

## Mode-invariant rules (all modes)

1. **Institutional calm.** The voice is never raised — even when the mode's copy "shouts," it shouts in structure (the government blend's V3), not in punctuation.
2. **Safety vocabulary in the company's own mouth** — what differs per mode is where the constraint points.
3. **No real-vendor verbatim, no impersonation-ready material** (real product names, real claims, real stats). Patterns, not quotes.
4. **Sincerity is the satire.** No winks, no self-aware jokes, no exaggeration — the dissonance does the work.
5. **Pass the mode check before copy lands** (each mode's check below).

## Mode 1 — civic-optimist (default)

### Hard rules

1. **Voice asymmetry is grammar law.** The system and its customers *do* (connect, respond, solve, deliver); the public *undergoes* (located, cleared, captured, retained). Never name the watched as an actor.
2. **Second person = the buyer.** "You" and "your team" address agencies; the public appears only in the third person, as beneficiaries.
3. **Numbers argue; hedges hide.** Big round figures carry persuasion in the headline; the qualifiers that gut them live in sub-clauses and small print.
4. **Concede everything except the conclusion.** Agree with critics' principles at length, then narrow the question until the product wins it.
5. **Never self-deprecate.** Jargon runs in one prestige direction — military, legal, compliance registers. No office-casual buzzwords.
6. **Sentence case, plain verbs, no filler** — obscurity lives in noun compounds, not abstraction.

### Stock sentence shapes (S1–S15)

| # | Shape | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **[Fragment]. [Fragment].** — verbless slogan pair | M |
| S2 | **Imperative triad** — Verb Fast. Verb More. Verb Ahead. | M |
| S3 | **"X requires more than Y. It needs Z."** — need reframe toward the product | M |
| S4 | **"Every X is [verb-ed]. Every Y is [adj]."** — anaphoric audit-flex | M |
| S5 | **"[Big number]+ [beneficiary] [passive verb] in [year]."** | M |
| S6 | **"[N] courts have [uniformity adverb] held that…"** — authority quantification | D |
| S7 | **Denominator inflation** — "roughly [tiny]% of [huge total]" | D |
| S8 | **Concession pivot** — "That doesn't mean [concession]. But [countervailing stat/reframe]." | D |
| S9 | **Question replacement** — "The real question isn't whether [strawman]. It's whether [preferred framing]." | D |
| S10 | **Capability disclaimer** — "does not and cannot [the feared thing], much less [worse thing]" | D |
| S11 | **Anti-silo pitch** — "[competitors] live in silos. [Product] is the glue that connects…" | M |
| S12 | **Comparative-adverb triad** — "operate smarter, respond faster, and stay connected" | M |
| S13 | **Governance-flex list** — past-tense "we've" + strengthening verbs | M |
| S14 | **FAQ demurral** — "Is [product] only for [narrow user]? No. [Product] supports a wide range of…" | M |
| S15 | **Antithesis chiasmus** — "Systems that are [bad] don't [improve]. Systems [like ours] can." | D |

Tag M = marketing surface, D = defensive surface.

### Lexicon (pattern banks)

- **Menace-laundering euphemisms**: watching becomes "real-time situational awareness"; capture becomes "point-in-time image"; tracking becomes "location data"; the net's limits become "guardrails," "safeguards," "clear boundaries"; scale becomes "connectivity," "interoperability"; totality becomes "the full picture."
- **Vague agency verbs**: respond, investigate, solve, clear, act fast, bring together, connect, locate, recover — effectiveness claimed, process unnamed.
- **Safety-optimism register**: "public safety" as the unchallenged master noun; "works better together"; "across jurisdictions"; "community expectations" as authority by proxy.
- **Compliance theater**: "every search is logged"; "transparency" as the stress-word; "accountability" aimed at users, never the vendor; certification names as prose; GDPR vocabulary worn as a shield.
- **Defensive register** (blog voice): "grounded in definitions, data, and facts"; "proportionality" via denominator math; "isolated instances" vs. pattern; "reasonable people can disagree" as closure; Claim/Fact headers.

### Where to push (satire leverage)

1. **Voice-asymmetry inversion** — make the watched the grammatical subject of the watcher's own verbs ("you will be located").
2. **Guardrail literalism** — the safeguard metaphor family pushed to its physical conclusion.
3. **Denominator comedy** — a tiny-percentage reassurance restated with its honest denominator, side by side.
4. **The small-print confession** — one sample-selecting qualifier carrying the joke inside a sincere quantified-reassurance block.
5. **Triad verb swap** — keep the imperative-triad rhythm, darker verbs (Notice. Record. Retain.).
6. **Claim/Fact parody** — a FAQ that concedes in the Claim and dissolves in the Fact via denominator math.
7. **Sunlit normalcy** — sincere copy about the darkest capability on the design language's warmest surfaces.

**Mode check**: Title Case, exclamation energy, named critics, raised voice, or a watched person acting → register drift; rewrite.

## Mode 2 — imperial

The register that sells surveillance as **inevitability**. One step past the civic-optimist's grammar: the watched appear *nowhere* — no beneficiary, no neighbor; people exist only as operators, "human validation," and data.

### Rules

1. **Imperium calm** — flat, certain, declarative; nothing hedged, nothing apologized for, nothing excited. Reassurance adjectives barely appear; certainty is structural.
2. **"You" = the institution.** Possessives bind the reader to their empire ("your data asset," "your operations"). No public at all.
3. **Abstractions do the acting.** The ontology integrates; the platform powers dominance. Humans are nodes the software augments — system-active, human-instrumental.
4. **Numbers are magnitude, rank, and speed — never human outcome.** "Thousands of Users. Millions of Sensors." / "Days, not years." / "No. 1." Scale is self-referential.
5. **Epochal self-framing.** Every product is "the operating system for [a domain]" — infrastructure of history, not a tool. ™ on the slogan.
6. **Authority is self-graded, then notarized** — analyst rankings curated in your own carousel, commander testimony, founder conviction, accreditation slash-lists. The register never argues with anyone.
7. **Capability vocabulary flaunted, guardrails sold as a capability tier** — "Granular AI Guardrails" beside the data layers at feature parity; compliance as slash-prefixed terminal output.

### Stock sentence shapes (P1–P15)

| # | Shape |
|---|---|
| P1 | **"The Operating System for [Domain] [Gerund]."** — totalizing genre claim |
| P2 | **Scalar triad** — "Thousands of Users. Millions of Sensors. A Single Pane of Glass." |
| P3 | **Verb-first imperative slogan** — "Get AI Into Operations." |
| P4 | **Epochal antithesis + ™** — "[Adjective] Noun of Tomorrow. [Verb]ed Today.™" |
| P5 | **Anaphoric connect-triad** — "Connect AI to Enterprise [Noun]." ×3 |
| P6 | **Stakes-confession pivot** — "models built in labs typically fail… — but that's where the stakes are highest." |
| P7 | **Reciprocal-loop claim** — the system improves itself through use |
| P8 | **Possessive-totality guarantee** — ownership "now and at every point in the future" |
| P9 | **"X, not Y" compression** — "Days, not years." |
| P10 | **Rank claim + receipt line** — "No. 1 …" — analyst citation |
| P11 | **Conviction quotation** — commander/CEO first-person belief |
| P12 | **Tenure + deep verb + gravitas list** — "For [duration], [product] has [verb]ed… and beyond." |
| P13 | **Feat-to-feat commitment sentence** — "From [extreme feat] to [extreme feat]…" |
| P14 | **Bold fragment as paragraph header** — "Collaboration, Supercharged." / "No Duplication." |
| P15 | **Slash-prefixed attestation list** — "/ SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001" |

### Lexicon (pattern banks)

- **Dominion & decision nouns**: "decision dominance," "operational edge," "single pane of glass," "full spectrum," "ontology," "closed-loop operations," "systems of action," "kinetic action layer," "integrated deterrence."
- **Operator verbs**: activate, drive, power, accelerate, surface, harness, wield, deter and defend.
- **Scale register**: "massive volumes of near-real-time data," "the enterprise," "all domains," "the most complex [domain] challenges," "the world's most important institutions."
- **Guardrail-as-capability**: "human validation," "all action can be audited," role/classification/purpose-based access chains, "operational truth."
- **Receipts**: "No. 1" + em-dash citation, ™, accreditation slash-lists, "embedded alongside" proximity.

**Where Plume uses it**: dark ops surfaces, platform "infrastructure" claims, security capability grids. Pair with the design skill's data-viz ramp and imperial-register wash discipline.

**Mode check**: a named beneficiary, a human outcome number, an apology, or an addressed critic → drift; rewrite.

## Mode 3 — plain speech

The mirror register: trust **earned by admission** instead of asserted by authority. Same institutional calm; opposite mechanic. Civic-optimist asserts, imperial declares, plain speech concedes.

### Rules

1. **Institutional candor.** Lead with admissions ("we aren't confident it will go well"-shaped); the concession is load-bearing, not decorative.
2. **We-first grammar, constraint pointed inward.** The company acts on its own technology and conduct — never on persons. "You" appears only in product surfaces.
3. **One adjective triad as identity tag**, repeated verbatim on every surface ("reliable, interpretable, and steerable"-shaped — invent the Plume triple, e.g. auditable / bounded / forgetful-class).
4. **Hedges headline.** Uncertainty vocabulary (believe, rough, plausible, scenarios, evidence) is prestige-bearing — humility worn as a credential.
5. **Caution in the second clause.** Benefit first, mitigation end-weighted — the slot the civic-optimist gives its stat.
6. **Fragment poverty.** Full sentences, sentence case everywhere; fragments survive only as CTAs and label constructions. No slogan pairs, no imperative triads, no Title Case.
7. **One exclamation point per long-form, spent on candor** — enthusiasm reserved for admitting you might be wrong.
8. **Low-pressure commerce.** Two-word imperative CTAs ("Try [Product]."), zero urgency; reassurance rides inline ("No commitment · Cancel anytime"-shaped); the honest asterisk as body text ("Usage limits apply."-shaped).

### Stock sentence shapes (A1–A14)

| # | Shape |
|---|---|
| A1 | **Plain declarative self-definition** — "[Company] is a [category] working to [triad clause]." |
| A2 | **Triad as identity tag** — the same triple, every surface |
| A3 | **Stakes admission** — "[big impact]… we aren't confident it will go well." |
| A4 | **Concede-then-believe** — "[objection]… Nevertheless, we believe [view]." |
| A5 | **Stewardship claim** — "We [restraint verb] [big abstraction]." |
| A6 | **End-weighted caution pair** — "securing its benefits and mitigating its risks" |
| A7 | **Two-word imperative CTA** — "Try [Product]." |
| A8 | **Imperative value title** — "Do the simple thing that works." |
| A9 | **Plain-idiom self-deprecation** — "no spaceship if a bicycle will do" |
| A10 | **Hedged section title** — "Our rough view on [contentious thing]." |
| A11 | **Scenario-carving** — "Let's carve this into three scenarios…" |
| A12 | **Empirical fallibilism** — "we'll change [X] if we see evidence…" |
| A13 | **Low-pressure pricing line** — "[Reassurance] · [Reassurance]." |
| A14 | **Honest asterisk** — "Usage limits apply." |

### Lexicon (pattern banks)

- **Trust triad + orbit**: the one repeated triple; trustworthy, secure, understandable — describing the company's *own* systems.
- **Stewardship verbs**: build, serve, secure, mitigate, guide, translate, share — verifiable company actions only.
- **Epistemic-humility vocabulary**: believe, evidence, rough, plausible, skeptical — rigor performed as candor.
- **Constraint-machinery proper nouns**: named artifacts of self-limitation (a trust, a policy, a constitution-shaped object) — parodyable by taking them literally.
- **Humanist abstractions / product-plainness**: "humanity's long-term well-being" register on values pages; homely commerce language on product pages — keep the two separated.

**Where Plume uses it**: governance essays, responsibility bands, the FAQ's stewardship half — especially surfaces wearing the warm creams, where the candor mechanic applied to a surveillance net ("we aren't confident it will go well") is quieter and darker than any triad.

**Mode check**: Title Case, verbless slogan fragments, big-round stat banners, urgency CTAs → drift back to the civic-optimist; rewrite.

## Mode 4 — Government register blend

The government-formal / brand-marketing blend, for **data-backed claim surfaces**. Its engine: **V1–V3 make claims, V4 prices them, V5 disclaims them** — serif promises, mono paperwork — and no voice ever acknowledges another.

### The five voices

- **V1 Consumer-retail**: "you" = the citizen; wellbeing as ease and affordability, never the harm itself; momentum by fragment; hedges only as upsell; even the 404 keeps the clerk voice.
- **V2 Prosecutorial indictment**: the country as victim in past progressive ("was being overcharged"); "the same" identity anaphora; the loudest number hiding its hedge ("up to 1000%"); verdict by two-word fragment ("This is unacceptable.").
- **V3 Presidential statement**: third-person office-as-agent ("the President has ensured"); era-ending declarations; "every" as totalization by grammar; authority by volume — the one loud voice. **Do not adopt the volume**: superlatives break the civic-optimist calm; borrow the *structure* (era-declaration, universal benefit) only into clearly-marked statement surfaces, never the default register.
- **V4 Public-notice / receipt (mono)**: nouns do the work, verbs are fired; the database logline ("showing 80 medications, sorted by Best deal (% Off)"-shaped); the world as control group ("Global reference"); two number regimes — mythic round serif vs. forensic mono cents, never in one sentence; deadpan self-audit ("Last updated unavailable.").
- **V5 Procurement-bureaucracy**: acronym-fluent question density; blunt negative-eligibility fragments ("[Population] excluded."); definition clauses ("One month is defined as…"); unilateral reservation; four-word disclaimers ("Eligibility and restrictions apply.").

### Stock sentence shapes (T1–T22)

| # | Shape | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **"X should be easy—and Y."** | V1 |
| T2 | **"Take control of your [noun] with the world's best [noun]."** | V1 |
| T3 | **"Now, with [quantity] of [thing]."** — momentum fragment | V1 |
| T4 | **"This is not the [expected thing]."** — denial-of-error 404 | V1 |
| T5 | **"If you have [system], check [thing] first—it may be even lower."** — hedge-as-upsell | V1 |
| T6 | **"This is an [pricing-status] price."** — bare disclosure | V1/V5 |
| T7 | **"[Country/group] was being [past-participle]."** | V2 |
| T8 | **"The same [X], [same-pp], [same-pp]…"** — identity anaphora | V2 |
| T9 | **"up to [absurd number]% more than in any other country."** | V2 |
| T10 | **"This is [moral adjective]."** — two-word verdict | V2 |
| T11 | **"the days of [villain]-[gerund] are over."** | V3 |
| T12 | **"[Gerund] the full [power-noun] of [institution], [office] has ensured [universal benefit]."** | V3 |
| T13 | **"the most [adjective] [noun] in the history of our country."** | V3 |
| T14 | **"[Audience]: showing [N] [items], sorted by [metric]."** — database logline | V4 |
| T15 | **"[Deal label] / [Benchmark label]"** — control-group label pair | V4 |
| T16 | **"[Floor] at $[X]. [Original] · [N]% off."** — receipt row grammar | V4 |
| T17 | **"[Big round number]+ [beneficiary passive-verb]."** — mythic chip | V4 |
| T18 | **"[Noun] unavailable." / "[Noun] may vary."** — deadpan absence | V4 |
| T19 | **"[Party] reserves the right to [withdraw anything]."** | V5 |
| T20 | **"[Unit] is defined as [unit]."** | V5 |
| T21 | **"[Population] excluded."** | V5 |
| T22 | **Compound program-mechanics question** — stacked acronyms, passive requirements | V5 |

### Where Plume uses it

The receipt/public-notice voice (V4 + V5) for data-backed satire surfaces — price ledgers for surveillance ("$0.00 / your location data · 100% off"-shaped), public-notice blocks, coupon-legal fine print under a sincere promise. Pair with the design skill's receipt/ledger motif and mono authority voice. V2's indictment and V3's statement are available only as clearly-marked standalone blocks (a "statement" section with the teleprompter reveal), never blended into civic-optimist-register copy.

**Mode check**: a superlative in the default register, a hedge meeting a mythic number in one sentence, or a verb in a ledger header → drift; rewrite.

## Usage discipline

- Copy is generated only when the effort calls for it; the prototype currently carries placeholder copy.
- **One surface, one mode** — pick from the selection table before writing; the blend is Mode 4's internal structure, not a mixing license.
- Every artifact passes its mode check and the invariant rules before it lands.
- The visual language does half the work — pair mode choice with the `plume-design-language` skill's motifs (receipt motif ↔ Mode 4; wash discipline ↔ Mode 2; triad repetition ↔ Mode 3) rather than loading satire into the words.
- This skill is self-contained: the lexicons above are the full working distillation; nothing downstream needs the raw research.
