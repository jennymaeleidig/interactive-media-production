FAQ

What is the Safe City Subscription?

Safe City is a long-term public safety subscription that gives cities every Flock product under one contract. It covers detection, investigation, and response tools across all municipal departments. Hardware and software updates are included automatically, and pricing is based on population. Cities get full deployment, support, and future-proof technology without starting a new RFP every time.

What’s included when we sign a Safe City contract?

Cities get immediate access to all Flock hardware and software, hands-on training, and a dedicated success team. We handle installation, community rollout, and ongoing support. You’ll receive regular performance reviews and annual reports showing measurable outcomes like faster response times, higher clearance rates, and improved officer and resident safety.

How does Safe City pricing work?

Safe City pricing scales with the amount of technology and coverage a city needs. Cities get full access to Flock technology on day one, then pay gradually over five to ten years with front-loaded discounts, predictable costs, and built-in upgrades at about the price of a coffee and bagel per citizen.

Why not just buy tools one at a time?

One-off procurement takes too long. Each new tool means another RFP, another approval cycle, and more delays. Safe City gives cities every Flock technology up front with a simple per-resident subscription. You pay over time, not all at once, and avoid years of waiting for the tools your officers and community need now.

How does Safe City keep our technology current?

Safe City eliminates the risk of obsolescence. All hardware is refreshed and all software upgraded automatically during the contract. Cities stay current with the latest AI and safety technology without needing new RFPs, approvals, or budget requests. It’s a sustainable model that evolves as technology advances, so your investment always delivers.
