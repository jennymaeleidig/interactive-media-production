Uncover and Connect: Taking Human Trafficking Investigations to the Next Level

Who it's for: 
Law Enforcement
 to watch

Human Trafficking Moves Fast. So Must the Response.

Watch as we bring together public safety experts to explore how better coordination, smarter data use, and cross-jurisdictional awareness are redefining what’s possible in human trafficking investigations. 

What You’ll Learn

  • The Landscape of Human Trafficking
    Hear as Dr. Robyn D. McDougle, Associate Dean of Research and Outreach at VCU, unpacks the history, trends, and tactical challenges in today’s human trafficking investigations.
  • Turning Technology into Action
    Virginia Commonwealth Attorney Makenzie K. Babichenko breaks down how modern-day technology and tools are powering smarter investigations and stronger prosecutions.‍
  • Building a Connected Pursuit
    Aidan Daily, former Dispatcher, Officer, and Crime Analyst from Prospect TX PD, will demonstrate how departments can link tips, vehicle movement, and location history to enrich investigations, without needing to add headcount.
