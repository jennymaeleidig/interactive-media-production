Ask The Experts: How to Prevent Crime, Not Just Respond to It

Who it's for: 
No items found.
34 minutes
 to watch

Retail property managers today face challenges like theft, vandalism, illegal dumping, and vehicle break-ins—issues that undermine tenant satisfaction and erode a sense of safety. While traditional security measures like cameras and guards often fall short—offering only reactive responses—there’s a smarter, more proactive way to protect your property.

We hosted an interactive Ask Me Anything (AMA) webinar with Flock Safety product leaders and former law enforcement investigators. We explored the latest security technologies—such as license plate readers, mobile security trailers, and alert-based response services—that empowered property managers to deter crime before it happened, streamline incident resolution, and build stronger partnerships with law enforcement.

Attendees brought their toughest security questions and real-world scenarios. Our experts provided insights, shared best practices, and helped participants turn their properties into safer, more welcoming places for tenants and customers alike.
