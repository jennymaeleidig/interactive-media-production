Reducing Traffic Deaths and Building a Safer City

Who it's for: 
Transportation/Municipal Officials
 to watch

What if your city could prevent crashes before they happen? That’s what the Town of Truckee, California, is striving for as it reimagines public safety with help from Flock Safety. In this recorded webinar with the National League of Cities, hear how Truckee uses proactive technology to reduce traffic-related fatalities and create a safer, more resilient community built on prevention and connection.

You’ll hear how Truckee’s Police Department partnered with city transportation teams to combine data, infrastructure improvements, and community trust into one coordinated safety effort. Learn what’s driving real progress, what’s changing on the ground, and how prevention-focused strategies can make streets safer for everyone. This conversation offers practical ideas and inspiration for any community focused on saving lives.
