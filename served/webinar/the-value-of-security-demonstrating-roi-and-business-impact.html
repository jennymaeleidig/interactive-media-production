Security is often viewed as a necessary expense or even a necessary evil, but leading organizations know it’s a measurable driver of enterprise value and profitability.

‍
In this session, Bonnie Michelman broke down how to quantify and communicate the true return on investment of your security program. Through real-world case examples and data-backed insights, she demonstrated how effective security operations reduce liability, prevent loss, protect brand reputation, and directly support profitability.

Attendees will learn how to:

  • Measure and articulate security ROI in financial terms executives understand
  • Showcase the impact of risk mitigation, loss prevention, and liability reduction
  • Reframe security as a strategic enabler – not a “necessary evil”
  • Communicate value in a way that earns executive buy-in and sustained investment

‍

Who should watch: Security, risk, and facilities professionals responsible for protecting people, assets, and operations, and who need to demonstrate the business value of their programs.

‍

Featuring

Bonnie Michelman, MBA, MS, CPP, CHPA
Security consultant, speaker, and principal of "Dynamic Security Strategies"

Bonnie Michelman is a nationally recognized security leader and former Chief Security Officer of Mass General Brigham, where she led security strategy across a 13-hospital system and hundreds of facilities. She brings decades of experience helping organizations quantify risk, reduce loss and liability, and position security as a driver of business value.
