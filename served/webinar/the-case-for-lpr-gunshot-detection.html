The Case for LPR + Gunshot Detection

Who it's for:

In 2020 gun-related deaths accounted for 45,222 deaths – the highest in 40 years. Watch our on-demand webinar to learn how agencies of any size can use LPR and gunshot detection devices in tandem. From detecting gun-related incidents quickly and accurately to saving minutes when lives are at risk to preserving crucial evidence – Flock’s system helps you address gun violence proactively.

‍

Flock Safety Solutions Consultant and former Lieutenant at Shelby County Sheriff's Office, David Ballard, and Customer Success Manager and former Real-Time Information Specialist at Elk Grove PD, Caity Peak, present:

‍

  • How to virtually "string the tape" before arriving at a crime scene
  • How to draw a virtual perimeter to identify vehicular leads proactively
  • How to effectively implement and scale gunshot detection and LPR in low and high-crime areas to create situational awareness
