FAQ

Besides a license plate number, what other vehicle details do Flock license plate reader cameras capture?

Vehicle Signature — standard on all Flock LPR cameras — uses machine learning to analyze captured footage to identify key details that traditional LPR cameras can’t, including:

  • Make
  • Body type (sedan, SUV, pickup truck, etc.)
  • Color
  • Plate state
  • Resident or non-resident vehicle
  • Type of plate (standard vs. temporary)
  • Missing plate
  • Other identifiers (roof rack, window stickers, toolbox, and more)

‍

What AI features do Flock's video cameras have?

Flock’s video cameras are equipped with advanced AI analytics features including Guardian Mode, People Detection Alerts, Visual Alerts and Flock FreeForm.

Do Flock license plate reader cameras capture personal or facial data?

No, Flock LPR cameras only capture vehicle data. They do not collect personal information, facial recognition, or anything tied to individuals.

Can I install video cameras and license plate reader cameras on the same pole?

Yes, both devices can be installed on the same pole.

What other advanced vehicle search capabilities does Flock offer?

Flock FreeForm expands on Vehicle Signature by going beyond preset filters, enabling searches based on everyday language, such as “white sports car with a racing stripe” or “red pickup truck with a dog in the bed.” It helps agencies and businesses find the exact vehicle they’re looking for fast.

Flock’s Enhanced LPR upgrade amplifies your LPR system with advanced analytics, real-time insights, and tools for multi-jurisdictional collaboration—making it easier to detect plate swaps, uncover organized crime, and connect cases faster.

Who handles the installation of the cameras?

Flock Safety takes care of installation, so you don't have to lift a finger. We’ll give you a heads up before our installers come onsite. We finalize camera locations before the installation visit, ensuring we are always well-prepared for a successful installation.

How are Flock's cameras powered?

Flock’s cameras come in AC or solar-powered options. Our team can help you select the best camera for your specific needs.

Who owns the data captured by Flock license plate reader cameras?

Ownership, title, and interest in the customer data belong to and are retained solely by our customers. Flock maintains a limited license to access the customer data for the sole purpose of providing our services. Flock does not own customer data and, as a result, does not maintain records of customer data after the retention period.
