Speakers

Meet the experts leading this year’s events.

Ambassador "Sully" Sullenberger
Keynote Speaker

Ambassador “Sully” Sullenberger has devoted his life to safety, serving as a pilot, safety expert, author, and U.S. Ambassador to the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). Nominated by President Biden and confirmed by the Senate in 2021, Sullenberger reasserted U.S. leadership at ICAO, addressing critical aviation issues like climate change and geopolitical disputes. After his ambassadorship, he resumed his role as a sought-after keynote speaker and advocate for global aviation safety.

Sullenberger’s career spans decades of service and leadership. A graduate of the United States Air Force Academy, he served as a fighter pilot and training officer before transitioning to commercial aviation, where he accrued over 20,000 flight hours. He became internationally renowned in 2009 for safely landing US Airways Flight 1549 on the Hudson River after a dual engine failure, a feat celebrated as the “Miracle on the Hudson.”

In addition to his flying achievements, Sullenberger is a #1 New York Times best-selling author of Highest Duty: My Search for What Really Matters, which inspired the critically acclaimed film Sully starring Tom Hanks. A native of Denison, Texas, he began flying at 16 and holds a bachelor’s degree, two master’s degrees, and honorary doctorates. He continues to champion leadership, crisis management, and the cause of democracy worldwide.

John Walsh
Keynote Speaker

John Walsh is an Emmy®-winning host and victims’ advocate best known for America’s Most Wanted, which helped law enforcement capture over 1,100 fugitives and recover more than 50 missing children during its 24-season run. Walsh’s life mission began in 1981 after the abduction and murder of his six-year-old son Adam. He and his wife, Revé, turned their tragedy into action by founding the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in 1984, which has since helped recover over 277,000 missing children and tackle online child exploitation through its CyberTipline.

Walsh has driven major child protection legislation, including the “Adam Walsh Child Protection and Safety Act” signed into law by President George W. Bush in 2006. Honored by law enforcement and U.S. presidents, he is one of only three people to be named an honorary U.S. Marshal. The Walsh family’s story, dramatized in NBC’s films Adam and Adam: His Song Continues, raised national awareness of child abduction and inspired change.

Walsh continues to fight for victims’ rights through television, writing, and public advocacy. He has authored three best-selling books and remains active with NCMEC alongside his wife, three children, and three grandchildren, working to protect children and support crime victims worldwide.

‍

Chief CJ Davis
Memphis PD

Memphis Police Chief Cerelyn “CJ” Davis began her 35 year career in law enforcement moving through the ranks at the Atlanta Police Department, with positions including Commander overseeing the Special Operations Section, Internal Affairs Commander, Airport Section Asst. Commander, and Deputy Chief. In 2016 she accepted the position of Chief of Police for the City of Durham, NC where she made great strides towards improving outreach and crime reduction efforts by building trust between citizens and law enforcement. In 2021, Chief Davis was selected by Memphis Mayor Jim Strickland as the city’s first female police chief.

Chief Davis is a graduate of the 225th Session of the National FBI Academy and completed senior management training at the Police Executive Research Forum (PERF) in Boston, MA. She is a past President of the National Organization of Black Law Enforcement Executives (NOBLE), and testified before the U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee on the matter of Police Reform. She serves on the Board of Directors for the International Association of Chiefs of Police (IACP) and was appointed to North Carolina Governor Roy Cooper’s Racial Equity in Criminal Justice Task Force.

‍

Sheriff John Allen
Bernalillo County Sheriff's Office

Sheriff John Allen has spent the entirety of his professional life dedicated to upholding law, order, and justice in his community. With a career spanning over two decades, Sheriff Allen has been an integral part of the law enforcement community of New Mexico. 

Sheriff Allen began his distinguished career in 1997 with the New Mexico State Police, stationed in Las Vegas, New Mexico. In 2001, he moved to the Bernalillo County Sheriff's Office (BCSO), where his commitment to serving the public led him to work in all area commands. His expertise and dedication quickly led him to the role of a detective, and later to a sergeant. Sheriff Allen has held supervisory and instructor positions, where he made significant contributions to the professional development of his colleagues and law enforcement officers. After retiring from BCSO in 2019, Sheriff Allen served as an Instructor at the Central New Mexico Community College Law Enforcement Academy in 2020. 

In January 2023, his career culminated in his election as Sheriff. In January 2024, Sheriff Allen became a member of Governor Lujan Grisham’s Organized Crime Commission. Sheriff Allen contributes actively to several committees. He is a valued member of the New Mexico National Alliance on Mental Illness Crisis Intervention Team committee, the Mothers Against Drunk Driving Honoring Heroes Committee, and serves as an executive board member for the Bernalillo County Criminal Justice Coordinating Council. In every role and every responsibility, Sheriff John Allen has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to serving his community, enhancing law enforcement's professionalism, and promoting justice. His career continues to be an exemplary model for future law enforcement officers in Bernalillo County and beyond.

‍

Retired Chief Mike Moulton
El Cajon PD

Mike Moulton recently retired as the Chief of Police for the City of El Cajon after a 29 year career in law enforcement. He began his career working various positions from the Community Policing Unit to becoming a detective in the Gang Suppression Unit, then a Sergeant, Lieutenant, and Captain managing units including patrol, internal affairs, training, and public information. He is an accomplished leader in strategic planning, crisis management, organizational leadership, and team building.

Ret. Chief Moulton holds a Master’s degree in Law Enforcement and Public Safety Leadership from the University of San Diego, and is a graduate of California POST’s Command College and the Police Executive Research Forum’s (PERF) Senior Management Institute for Police.

‍

Frank Sorgie
Academy Sports + Outdoors

Frank Sorgie serves as the Senior Manager of Loss Prevention at Academy Sports + Outdoors, a premier sporting goods and outdoor retailer in the United States. With a tenure beginning in 2018, Frank has been instrumental in developing and implementing strategies to mitigate theft and enhance store security. His proactive approach has significantly reduced shrinkage and bolstered the safety of both customers and employees.

In response to the rising challenge of organized retail crime, Frank spearheaded the integration of Flock Safety’s advanced license plate recognition technology across multiple store locations. This initiative not only deterred potential criminal activities but also strengthened collaboration with local law enforcement agencies. Frank’s leadership in leveraging innovative security solutions underscores his commitment to maintaining a secure and welcoming environment for all patrons.

‍

Bobby Mitchell
Hawthorne PD

Lt. Mitchell has served the Hawthorne Police Department in California since 2003, bringing over two decades of experience in law enforcement. Beginning as a narcotics investigator, he was promoted to sergeant in 2011, where he took on key roles in Jail/Desk/Property, Patrol, and Training. Advancing to lieutenant in 2020, he has led critical initiatives as a Patrol Watch Commander, Detective Bureau Commander, and the driving force behind the establishment of the Real-Time Intelligence Center (RTIC) and Drone as First Responder (DFR) program, which he continues to develop.

His leadership and commitment to innovation have significantly contributed to advancing public safety in Hawthorne, a city in the Los Angeles metropolitan area, located in southwestern Los Angeles County, California.

‍

Eugene Kim
Beverly Hills PD

Lt. Eugene Kim serves as the Technology Services Bureau Commander at the Beverly Hills Police Department.  With over 25 years of law enforcement experience, he currently manages the Beverly Hills PD Real Time Watch Center (RTWC) and the in-house Digital Forensics Task Force. Some of his past assignments have been fraud investigator, narcotics K9 handler, field training officer, and Federal Task Force Officer.  As a Sergeant he supervised the US Secret Service Westside High Tech Crime Task Force based out of Beverly Hills PD. 

He is a former digital forensics examiner and has extensive knowledge of electronic surveillance. Lt. Kim served as the past region chair for Los Angeles County with the California Peace Officer’s Association (CPOA) and still sits on the board for the Los Angeles Organized Retail Crime Association (LA ORCA). He regularly instructs on various topics to both law enforcement and commercial technology firms. Lt. Kim has been a past keynote speaker at conferences in the US and overseas. Some of his past presentations in 2024 include, CPOA Law and Legislation Panel and testimony before the CA State Assembly Subcommittee, National Real Time Crime Center Association (NRTCCA) Conference, and webinars with the National White Collar Crime Center (NWC3). 

‍

Garrett Langley
Flock Safety

Garrett Langley is a technology entrepreneur dedicated to the bold mission of eliminating crime. He founded and leads Flock Safety, the first all-in-one technology platform pioneering a new approach to public safety—prioritizing efficacy, affordability, transparency, and accountability. The idea for Flock Safety emerged after Garrett experienced crime in his own neighborhood and realized existing security solutions failed to provide actionable evidence.

An electrical engineer and serial entrepreneur, Garrett co-founded Flock Safety in 2017 alongside Matt Feury and Paige Todd to transform public safety technology with advanced innovation and a community-first approach. Previously, he helped build and scale Clutch, a car subscription service, and Experience, a mobile-first fan platform, which he led to a $200 million acquisition by Cox Enterprises in 2014.

Garrett serves on multiple boards at his alma mater, Georgia Tech, supporting innovation and economic development. He is also a member of the Metro Atlanta Chamber 2023 Board of Directors and the Board of the High Museum. He lives in Atlanta with his wife, Megan, their children, and their dog.

‍

Bailey Quintrell
Flock Safety

Bailey Quintrell is Chief Strategy Officer at Flock Safety. In this role, he oversees the Solution Engineering teams, Product Management and Design, strategic partnerships and the go-to-market strategy of emerging products.

Bailey joined Flock Safety as an early employee in 2018, one year after the company was founded. He was brought on to launch and lead the company’s law enforcement vertical, which today is the highest revenue-generating vertical at Flock. Prior to his current role, Bailey led a number of initiatives across the company including starting the Strategy and External Affairs teams.

Prior to joining Flock Safety, Bailey worked first in engineering and then technical and channel sales roles at several industrial and energy companies, including MWM GmbH which was acquired by Caterpillar, Inc. Bailey graduated from the Georgia Institute of Technology with a degree in Mechanical Engineering. He lives in Atlanta, Georgia with his family.
