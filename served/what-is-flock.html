What is Flock

We build technology that helps law enforcement, businesses, schools, and communities find relevant information, respond faster, and make better-informed decisions. Privacy controls, transparency tools, and local control are built into how the system works.

A clearer picture of Flock.

Flock is a connected public safety platform that helps authorized users find information relevant to an incident. Flock LPR is one part of that platform, providing vehicle data for investigations.

Reality

What Flock LPR does do

Misconception

What Flock LPR does not do

Record vehicle sightings at a specific place and time

Flock does not identify people by their faces

Capture license plates and vehicle characteristics

Flock does not identify who is inside a vehicle

Help authorized users find relevant vehicle evidence

Flock does not continuously track people or vehicles

Provide leads for investigators to assess alongside other evidence

Flock does not replace investigators or human judgment

Flock customers cannot access a live video feed from an LPR camera

Powerful technology needs clear boundaries.

Flock is designed to help authorized users find relevant evidence for investigations without compromising safety and compliance.
‍
Clear safeguards define how information is stored, accessed, shared, and reviewed. Customers own their data and control how it is used under their policies and applicable law.

7-day deletion by default

Images are deleted after 7 days by default, unless law or an authorized investigation requires otherwise.

Every search is logged

Searches are tied to a user and recorded for review.

Customer-controlled sharing

Customers decide what data is shared and with whom.

Encrypted by default

Data is encrypted so only authorized users can access it.

No ICE contracts

Flock does not contract with ICE or Palantir.

Customer-owned data

Customers own their data and control access.

Transparency portals

Agencies can make policies and usage information available to their communities.

Safer communities require useful evidence and public trust.

Flock builds public safety technology that helps agencies locate missing people, recover stolen vehicles, and investigate crime more quickly by helping them search for evidence relevant to an authorized investigation. Flock supports human decision-making. It does not make investigative conclusions or take enforcement action.

Flock LPR cameras capture vehicle events, including license plates, vehicle characteristics, and the time and location a vehicle passed a camera. That information helps agencies identify vehicles relevant to an investigation using Flock searches, alerts, and approved data sources they choose to enable, in accordance with their policies, local laws, and community values.

Flock connects incident detection, evidence search, and response tools in one platform. Depending on the products a customer uses, Flock can help teams identify relevant vehicles, review video footage, receive alerts, and build a clearer picture of an incident.

Communities use Flock to help locate missing people, recover stolen vehicles, investigate serious crime, and support faster emergency response. As public safety agencies face growing demands with limited resources, Flock helps investigators spend less time searching for information and more time responding to the people who need them.

The Flock Camera Map shows participating agencies and, where those agencies choose to make it available, general deployment and sharing information for authorized investigations.

Flock does not use facial recognition technology. Instead, Flock cameras focus on vehicle events to help investigators search for vehicles relevant to an authorized investigation.

Data is automatically deleted after 7 days by default, unless a different retention period is required by the customer or applicable law, or the customer preserves information as part of an authorized investigation.

Customers own their data.
Flock provides the infrastructure, but customers control access, sharing, and retention according to applicable laws and policies. Flock does not sell customer data or share it without authorization. Every search requires a documented reason and is logged for review.
