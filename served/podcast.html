About the Show

Behind every high-stakes moment are people, tech, and decisions made in seconds. That’s what this show is about. Dalton Webb and Jamie Hudson have lived it, led it, and now they’re talking through it — from crime center strategy to the small wins that drive big impact. If you’re curious how real-time policing works (or how to make it work better), you’re in the right place.
