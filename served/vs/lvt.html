Flock vs LVT

Mobile Security That Turns Insight Into Action

Flock unifies mobile security trailers, license plate readers (LPR), video cameras, drones, and audio detection into one platform designed to help you prevent crime, generate evidence, and work seamlessly with law enforcement. LVT’s business is built around solar-powered security trailers and basic deterrence.

Average

Flock vs. LVT at a Glance

Flock Safety

LVT

Platform Approach

An end-to-end security ecosystem: mobile trailers, LPR, video, drones, and Gunshot Detection unified in a single platform.

Primarily solar-powered surveillance trailers with live monitoring. Other capabilities typically require additional systems or integrators.

Crime Solving vs. Deterrence

Built to help you actually solve crime. Combine deterrence with evidence: powerful search tools, license plate data, and video to help identify suspects and support investigations.

Designed first around visual deterrence and live monitoring. Strong at making cameras visible, but with more limited investigation tools.

Total Cost & Pricing Transparency

Simple, transparent subscription pricing. Fewer surprise add-ons and clearer long-term total cost of ownership, especially as you scale locations and monitoring.

Pricing could become complex as you add features, monitoring, data, and integrations. Costs may rise as deployments grow.

Vendor & Support Model

One partner for trailers, LPR, video, and support. A single team helps you design deployments, handle installation, and support your operations.

Often sold and supported through channel partners and integrators, each with their own contracts and processes.

User Experience & Access to Evidence

An intuitive platform that your team can learn quickly. Security operators can immediately view, search, and export video and evidence themselves without waiting for manual pulls.

Accessing recorded video can be slower and more manual, frequently requiring a request and waiting for footage to be retrieved.

Law Enforcement Collaboration

Built to work hand-in-hand with law enforcement partners. Designed to make it easy to share relevant evidence in a controlled, auditable way when incidents occur.

Primarily focused on commercial properties and deterrence, with less emphasis on law enforcement workflows.

Adaptive Security Platform

Trailers plus the ability to layer on additional video cameras, LPR, audio, and other sensors over time, all managed in one platform.

Trailer-first model, so scaling coverage typically means adding more trailers and more field hardware.

Why Choose Flock

One Integrated Platform

You need a true crime-solving platform, not just another camera feed.

Flock's Solution

Flock brings trailers, license plate recognition, video, drones, and audio detection together in a single, unified platform. Teams get one place to see activity across locations, move seamlessly between devices, and turn footage into a complete investigative story.

Challenge with

LVT

LVT delivers a surveillance trailer focused on deterrence, leaving teams with a limited ability to turn video into actionable evidence.

01

Predictable, Long-Term Value

Plan your budgets with confidence instead of reacting to unexpected fees.

Flock's Solution

Flock’s subscription model is designed for clarity from day one. With transparent pricing, straightforward contracts, and fewer surprise add-ons as you scale, teams can budget confidently and plan for the future

Challenge with

LVT

LVT may look affordable at first, but add-on fees, reseller markups, and variable monitoring costs could make long-term spend harder to predict as deployments grow.

02

Built for Law Enforcement Collaboration

Do more than observe incidents. Act on them.

Flock's Solution

Flock is purpose-built to help you collaborate with law enforcement when and how you choose. Sharing clear, relevant evidence is fast and frictionless to push investigations forward and support lasting crime reduction.

Challenge with

LVT

LVT is built to capture incidents – not advance investigations. The platform offers limited workflows for sharing footage with law enforcement in order to quickly close cases.
