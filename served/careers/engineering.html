Product with Purpose

At Flock, we move fast. We make quick, informed decisions when the cost of being wrong is low, and apply rigor where it matters most: in our hardware, in our data and in the trust our customers place in us.

Our product and engineering teams are united by a shared drive to build what matters. We care about solving the hardest, most meaningful problems with speed, clarity and purpose. That means knowing when to push, when to pause and how to balance urgency with long-term impact.

We work cross-functionally, obsess over the details and never lose sight of the bigger picture. With sharp product sense, deep technical expertise and strong business context, we ship fast, own outcomes, and design systems that scale because what we build today becomes the foundation for tomorrow.

Discover Where You'll Build Best

Aviation

The Aviation team is responsible for building world class remotely piloted aircraft and supporting ground systems for public safety customers.  To give first responders an eye on the scene, we build flying cameras (drones) that push the boundaries of connectivity, video transmission, power and thermal management, and imaging.  We build platforms that are meant to fly thousands of missions without human intervention in harsh environments across the country.  We deliver these drones to our customers as a service and ensure they are able to operate them with safety and compliance without the need to become aviation experts themselves.

Device

In Device Engineering at Flock, we design and productize intelligent, connected devices for smart cities and public safety, leveraging cutting-edge AI, imaging, and audio hardware/software systems to enhance community and business safety. Our team tackles complex technological challenges, from writing embedded firmware for microcontrollers to developing edge AI solutions on advanced processors, while innovating in acoustic design, thermal management, and high-speed hardware systems. We create custom Android apps, optimize imaging for diverse outdoor conditions, and engineer durable electro-mechanical enclosures for rugged environments. Our R&D focuses on energy-efficient solutions, harnessing the latest solar technologies for scalable, infrastructure-free products. Every project delivers measurable, positive real-world impact, offering diverse challenges for passionate engineers.

Imaging

Our Imaging team designs and builds the next generation of high-performance camera and audio systems that power mission-critical public safety and commercial security solutions. From end-to-end hardware and software development, we build optics, sensors, embedded software, and real-time image processing. We integrate AI and computer vision directly into our pipeline for features like intelligent exposure control and adaptive scene detection. We optimize for real-world lighting, movement, and audio variability to deliver professional results in the outside world. Imaging is a deeply cross-functional team that creates scalable, reliable imaging systems from LPR cameras to audio-visual fusion platforms that deliver clarity, speed, and impact when it matters most.

Machine Learning

The Machine Learning team develops the statistical models, inference pipelines, and analysis systems to allow Flock to effectively deliver and maintain products that decode captured data into searchable information and actionable insights, allowing our customers to create safer communities.

Product Design

We’re building the first operating system for public safety, and design is at the core of everything we do. Our team transforms complex, high-stakes workflows, like CAD integrations, ALPR alerts, and multi-agency collaboration, into intuitive, responsive tools used by officers, investigators, and agencies across the country. Designers here lead end to end, driving research, strategy, UX, and UI to create human-centered products that are accessible and impactful. Our work helps solve crimes, protect communities, and support people in critical moments. We care deeply about craft, thrive on turning complexity into clarity, and grow together through direct feedback, collaboration, and shared wins.

Product Management

At Flock, product managers are helping solve real world problems that make communities, cities, and businesses safer. Product at Flock encompasses being able to deeply understand challenges at the customer level and translate that into measurable business value, all the way through to collaborating closely with engineers on game-changing solutions. Whether you're spending time in the field watching customers track suspect vehicles in real-time or deep diving into organized retail crime patterns, your days will be full of meaningful work that drives real impact.

Program Management

Our company builds highly complex products that span every engineering discipline, and Technical Program Managers (TPMs) sit at the center of it all. At Flock, TPMs speak the language of engineers, product managers, and business leaders alike. They drive clarity, momentum, and alignment by ensuring teams stay focused, communicate openly, and continuously improve. TPMs remove blockers, fill in the gaps, and keep us moving toward the next milestone, all while applying just enough process to keep us fast and flexible.

Software

At Flock, we process and analyze more than a billion images, sounds, and video clips daily. Our unwavering commitment to putting customers first drives us to leverage cloud and mobile technologies to combat crime on a large scale. The software teams at Flock utilize Go, Python, and TypeScript with Node and React to develop systems deployed on Kubernetes. The aim of our software teams is to actively listen to our customers and develop impactful tools that serve the community, all within a dynamic and fast-paced environment.
