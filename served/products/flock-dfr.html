FAQ

What is Flock DFR and how does it work?

Flock DFR is a fully remote Drone as First Responder system that launches automatically in response to 911 calls. It provides real-time video of the scene within an average of 86 seconds, giving officers situational awareness before they arrive. The system integrates with your existing tech stack, like CAD and LPR, enabling fast, automated aerial response — all without requiring rooftop staff.

How will this benefit my agency?

Agencies using Flock DFR report faster response times (up to 71% improvement), fewer unnecessary patrol deployments, and safer resolutions. It helps locate suspects or vehicles 89% more often and clears 20% of calls without sending an officer, reducing risks and freeing up resources.

Are there multiple options available?

Yes. Flock DFR supports multiple hardware setups to fit your agency’s needs — including the fastest contact-charging dock on the market and the only battery-swapping dock purpose-built for public safety. These systems maximize uptime and ensure drones are always mission-ready.

What kind of support and training does Flock provide?

Flock offers a turnkey deployment experience — from site planning and regulatory guidance to hands-on training and ongoing white-glove support. Whether you're launching your first drone program or scaling an existing one, our team ensures you’re operational, confident, and compliant from day one.

How is this different from a traditional drone program?

Unlike traditional drone operations that require the drone to be launched on scene and piloted by the responding officer, Flock DFR is always ready. It launches without human intervention, flies farther and longer, and gets to incidents first. It's faster, safer, and more scalable — offering real-time visibility and measurable improvements in response times and case resolution.

Is this a surveillance tool?

No. Flock DFR only activates in response to specific calls for service — never for general patrol or surveillance. Every flight is logged, audit-traceable, and visible via a public-facing transparency dashboard, ensuring responsible use and public trust.

What if we already have drones?

DFR will complement your existing drone program, taking your response to the next level. Traditional drone programs can't match the speed or coverage of Flock DFR. With automated launch, full tech integration, and 24/7 availability, Flock DFR is a true force multiplier.

How does Flock DFR integrate with our existing systems?

Flock DFR is built to fit seamlessly into your agency’s workflow. It integrates with CAD, LPR, gunshot detection, and more. These integrations enable faster dispatch, smarter response, and less friction for your team — all while reducing manual tasks.
