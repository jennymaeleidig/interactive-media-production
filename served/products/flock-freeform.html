How FreeForm Works

FreeForm combines powerful AI, cross-agency access and real-time alerts into one intuitive experience.

Designed to Help Teams Move Faster

FreeForm brings natural-language search, unified video and LPR workflows, permission-based shared camera access, and configurable alerts into one streamlined experience.

Search With Natural Language

Type what you’re looking for, such as “man in blue shirt and cowboy hat” or “dark SUV with rear bumper damage.” Then review relevant visual matches across enabled video feeds, with vehicle results also available from LPR systems.

Video and LPR, All in One Place

Review person-related video search results from enabled video feeds alongside vehicle results from video and LPR systems in one interface.

Search Beyond Your Own Cameras

When authorized and where sharing is enabled, search shared camera footage from participating agencies, campuses, businesses, or neighboring properties to help identify leads across approved partner networks.

Get Alerts When Your Suspect Appears

When alerts are enabled, authorized teams can receive notifications for potentially relevant matches so they can assess leads without having to watch every feed continuously.

Built for Every Role

From patrol response and investigations to GSOCs, RTCCs, retail asset protection, and enterprise security operations, FreeForm helps teams review evidence and coordinate next steps more efficiently.

Built-In Safeguards

Moderation tools, permission-based access controls, search history, and audit logs help agencies and organizations support responsible use and review searches when needed.

FAQ

How is FreeForm different from traditional video search tools?

Most video search tools rely on preset filters, such as vehicle color, vehicle type, clothing type, or timestamps. But investigations and security incidents often begin with incomplete information that doesn’t fit neatly into dropdown menus. FreeForm lets teams search using natural-language descriptions like: “woman in a black dress and cowboy hat”to review relevant visual matches across video and LPR evidence in one workflow.

What kind of training is required?

FreeForm is designed for a short learning curve. Teams can typically get started with a brief walkthrough or demo.

Who can use FreeForm?

FreeForm is built for authorized users across:

  • law enforcement
  • investigations
  • RTCCs
  • GSOCs
  • retail asset protection
  • campus security
  • healthcare security
  • enterprise security operations

It’s designed to support day-to-day workflows, investigations, real-time operations, and cross-team collaboration.

Does FreeForm people search use facial recognition?

No. FreeForm does not use facial recognition or biometric person recognition. Searches involving people are based on observable, non-biometric visual characteristics captured on enabled video cameras and video feeds, such as clothing and visible accessories. They are not designed to identify a specific person.

Will FreeForm work with our existing cameras and systems?

FreeForm works with:

  • Flock LPR cameras
  • Flock video cameras
  • Shared Flock camera networks
  • Compatible third-party video integrations are supported when setup compatibility requirements are met.

Availability depends on system setup and whether relevant cameras or video feeds are enabled for FreeForm features.

Can I use FreeForm search outside my organization or jurisdiction?

Only when authorized. Organizations and agencies can request permission-based access to shared video footage and license plate reader data from participating partners where sharing is enabled and consistent with applicable policies.

What does Flock FreeForm do?

Flock FreeForm lets authorized teams search enabled video and license plate reader (LPR) data using natural-language descriptions. Users can type details such as clothing, visible accessories, vehicle type, color, or damage and review relevant matches across video and LPR evidence in one workflow.

Will people search work on LPR cameras?

No. People search and related alerts are only available on enabled video cameras and video feeds.
