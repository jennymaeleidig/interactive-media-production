FAQ

How does Flock’s subscription model work?

Flock’s all-in-one subscription makes it affordable to get started. One simple annual price covers hardware, software, maintenance, and support with no hidden fees. Optional features, such as alert response service, can be added as needed.

Can the trailer be moved easily between sites or hotspots?

Yes. It’s designed to be redeployed quickly. Whether you’re rotating coverage or responding to new risks, you can move the trailer as needed without special equipment or setup.

Which applications are these trailers best suited for?

Flock’s mobile trailers are built for flexible, high-impact use across situations and industries. They’re ideal for parking lots, retail centers, construction sites, public events, critical infrastructure, emergency response scenarios (like natural or man-made disasters), and anywhere you need visible deterrence and alerts without permanent installation.

Do I need power, Wi-Fi, or permits?

No power or Wi-Fi needed — the trailer is fully solar-powered and LTE-connected. In most cases, permits aren’t required, so you can deploy quickly without red tape.

How can I collaborate with law enforcement using this system?

Flock makes it easy to securely share your cameras with over thousands partner agencies that are already on the platform. You can loop in law enforcement in real time to support faster, more coordinated responses.

Does Flock offer live remote video monitoring?

With Flock’s optional Alert Response Service, trained agents monitor live alerts from cameras and mobile trailers. When a person is detected in a restricted area or after hours, operators quickly verify the threat, issue audio warnings, and escalate to law enforcement or security as needed. It’s a cost-effective way to deter crime and respond faster — without adding on-site staff.
