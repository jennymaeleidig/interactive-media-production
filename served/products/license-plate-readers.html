A license plate recognition (LPR) camera captures license plates and key vehicle characteristics, such as make, color, and body type, to help generate investigative leads. Organizations use LPR technology to locate stolen vehicles, find missing people, investigate crimes, and respond to security incidents more effectively.

Flock combines purpose-built LPR cameras with connected vehicle intelligence, the nation's largest LPR network, and built-in accountability tools. That means organizations can securely share vehicle evidence, uncover more investigative leads, and work across jurisdictions while maintaining control over their data.

Flock LPR is used by public and private entities including law enforcement agencies, businesses, neighborhoods, apartment complexes, houses of worship, and schools. Organizations can use Flock independently or securely share relevant vehicle evidence with authorized law enforcement when it supports an investigation.

Privacy and accountability are built into the Flock LPR by design. Access is limited to authorized users, every search is logged and reviewable, and customers control their own data retention and sharing settings. Flock is designed to help organizations use vehicle intelligence responsibly while maintaining public trust. Learn more here.

Flock LPR helps organizations recover stolen vehicles, locate missing or endangered people, investigate violent and property crimes, and identify vehicles connected to security incidents.
