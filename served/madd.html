A Mission To End Impaired Driving

we’re making roads safer, together

Flock Safety and Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD) have joined forces to take a stand against impaired driving and hit-and-run crimes nationwide. By combining mission-driven advocacy with cutting-edge technology, this partnership empowers communities to save lives and deliver justice.

Stacey D. STewart

CEO, MADD

Flock Safety provides license plate recognition technology, allowing law enforcement to accurately and timely identify vehicles involved in incidents such as hit-and-run crashes. We believe that any technology that increases post-crash responsiveness and accountability will help to raise awareness and change behaviors to avoid impaired driving.”

Technology That Helps Keep Communities Safer From Drunk Driving

Every drunk driving crash is preventable. And when it comes to saving lives, every second matters. Flock Safety’s Wrong Way Detection and Crash Detection tools give law enforcement critical insight when and where it’s needed most. Wrong Way Detection alerts officers the moment a vehicle enters a road in the wrong direction, a dangerous behavior often linked to impairment. Crash Detection identifies when a collision occurs, even in areas where no one is around to call for help. By helping agencies respond faster and identify high-risk driving in real time, these tools make it possible to prevent more harm and protect more lives on the road.
